Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Repe works without one, and everything stays on your phone.

How do I change my plan? Plan tab, then Edit. You can also change a set's weight or reps right in the workout.

Why does the bar show those plates? Repe loads the bar from the plates at your gym. Change them in Settings, Plates at your gym.

How does the streak work? It counts weeks. Hit your weekly target and the week counts. Rest days never break it.

The rest timer doesn't buzz on time on Android. Allow Settings, Apps, Repe, Alarms & reminders. Repe also offers it in its Settings, under Reminders.

How do I delete my data? Delete the app. Nothing is kept anywhere else (privacy policy).
