Load the bar. Every week.

Repe is a workout tracker for people who lift in a gym. Your plan in two taps, every set pre-filled from last time, and a streak that counts weeks, not days.

  • Today's workout is ready when you walk in: one set at a time, tap Done, rest, repeat.
  • The bar shows the plates to load on each side, from your gym's plates.
  • Beat last time and the weight goes up when you've earned it.
  • Hit your weekly target and the streak grows. A rest day never breaks it.

Free, no ads, no account. Everything stays on your phone.
