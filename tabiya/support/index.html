Support

A question, a bug or an idea: write to devkiperszmid@gmail.com. Tell us your phone's model and system version, and what you did just before the problem. You'll get an answer from the person who makes the app.

Common questions

How does training work?

Each day Tabiya picks the moves that are due, oldest first, and adds a few new ones. Play each move on the board. A right move comes back later each time: in 1, 3, 9, 21, then 60 days. A missed move comes back at the end of the session and again tomorrow. Only your side's moves are trained.

How do I add an opening?

In Repertoire, tap Add an opening. Pick one from the catalog, open a PGN file, or paste a Lichess study link. You can also share a PGN file to Tabiya from any app.

What is the Lichess token for?

Lichess's opening explorer only answers apps that send a token. With one, Tabiya shows the common replies your repertoire doesn't cover yet, and can spar with the replies Lichess players choose most. Create a token on lichess.org (it needs no permissions), copy it and paste it into Tabiya. It stays on your phone and is only sent to Lichess.

The reminder comes a few minutes early or late (Android)

Android sends reminders when it suits the battery. To get it at the exact time, turn on Settings › Apps › Tabiya › Alarms & reminders.

I don't get the reminder at all

Check that notifications are on for Tabiya in your phone's settings, and that the reminder has a time in Progress › Daily reminder. It skips days you've already trained.

Does Tabiya need the internet?

No. Training, exploring your lines and playing the engine all work offline. Only the Lichess features need a connection.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Tabiya has no account or sync. Your phone's own backup (iCloud, or your Google account on Android) can include Tabiya's data, so restoring that backup on a new phone can bring your progress with it.

How do I start an opening over?

Open the opening in Repertoire, tap the menu and choose Reset progress. Every move becomes new again. Your streak and past sessions stay.
