Learn your chess openings by playing them.

Tabiya trains your repertoire on the board. Each day it picks the moves that are due. You play them. A miss comes back until it sticks.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

Spaced repetition, on the board

A move you play right comes back in 1, 3, 9, 21, then 60 days. A move you miss comes back at the end of the session and again tomorrow.

Your repertoire, White or Black

Start from a ready-made opening, or bring your own: a PGN file or a Lichess study. Only your side's moves are trained.

Explore, then spar

Walk your lines with the author's comments. Then play the opening against Stockfish, which runs on your phone, offline.

Private by default

No account, no ads, no analytics. Your openings and progress stay on your phone. Privacy policy
